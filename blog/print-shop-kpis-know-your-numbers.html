Know Your Numbers: 8 KPIs Every Print Shop Should Track

The metrics that show whether your print business is really healthy, and how to track them without a spreadsheet marathon.

By the Pulse Print team·November 4, 2026·2 min read

Ask a print shop owner how the business is doing and you'll often hear "busy". Busy isn't the same as profitable. To run a print shop with confidence you need a few clear numbers you can check every week. Here are the eight print shop KPIs that matter most.

1. Job margin

Revenue minus the real cost of materials, press time, labour and outsourcing, per job. Margin by product and by customer shows which work actually makes money and which only keeps you busy.

2. On-time delivery rate

The share of jobs delivered by the promised date. It is the KPI customers feel most directly. Track the reasons behind late jobs: waiting on proof, stock, press time or finishing.

3. Press utilisation

How much of each press's available time is spent producing saleable work, versus idle, make-ready or maintenance. Low utilisation on one press and overtime on another is a scheduling problem you can solve.

4. Waste and spoilage rate

Spoiled sheets as a percentage of sheets used, per press and product. Our guide on reducing production waste shows how to bring it down.

5. Turnaround time

Average time from order (or approved proof) to delivery. Faster turnaround wins work, as long as it doesn't come at the expense of margin or quality.

6. Quote win rate and quote speed

What share of quotes turn into orders, and how quickly you reply. Slow quotes lose jobs; consistently low win rates on a product can mean you're priced wrong.

7. Revenue per employee

A simple measure of productivity across the whole team. It shows whether new hires or new equipment are actually increasing output.

8. Repeat customer rate

The share of revenue from returning customers. Repeat clients are cheaper to serve and easier to grow, and reorder reminders help keep them.

How to track print shop KPIs without spreadsheets

Most shops struggle with KPIs because the data is scattered: quotes in one tool, tickets on paper, press times on a whiteboard. When everything runs through one print shop management software platform, these numbers update by themselves.

  • Pick three KPIs to start: margin, on-time delivery and waste are a strong trio.
  • Review them weekly with your team, not just at month end.
  • Agree one action per week based on what the numbers show.
