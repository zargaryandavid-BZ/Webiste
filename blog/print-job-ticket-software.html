Print Job Ticket Software: From Paper Tickets to an Automated Workflow

Paper tickets get lost, smudged and outdated. Digital job tickets keep every spec, file and deadline in one place, and move the job forward on their own.

By the Pulse Print team·November 4, 2026·2 min read

The job ticket is the backbone of every print shop. It tells prepress what to check, the press operator what to run, and finishing what to cut, fold or bind. Yet in many shops it is still a sheet of paper, or a PDF someone forgot to update. Print job ticket software turns that ticket into a live record that the whole team can see and trust.

The problem with paper job tickets

  • They get lost. A ticket left on the wrong desk can stop a job for hours.
  • They go out of date. A customer changes the quantity by email, but the ticket on the press still shows the old number.
  • They're retyped. Specs copied from the quote by hand are an open invitation to mistakes.
  • Nobody sees the big picture. You can't tell at a glance how many jobs are waiting on proofs, stock or press time.

What digital job tickets do differently

Created automatically from the quote

When a customer accepts a quote, the job ticket is generated with every spec already filled in: product, quantity, stock, size, colours, finishing, delivery and deadline. Nobody types anything twice. This is the first step most shops automate, as explained in our guide to print shop automation software.

Linked to the approved artwork

The ticket points to the exact file version the customer approved, so operators never pick the wrong one.

Tracked through every stage

Each job moves through clear stages, for example prepress, proof, approved, on press, finishing, ready and delivered. Everyone can see where it is, and customers can be updated automatically.

Updated in one place

If anything changes, it changes on the ticket, and everyone sees the new version immediately, on a desktop, a tablet at the press or a phone on the shop floor.

A live production board

Once tickets are digital, they can be shown together on a production board. Managers see what is on each press, what is next and which jobs are at risk of missing their deadline. Rush jobs can be slotted in with full awareness of what they will push back. Add AI scheduling and the board can even suggest the best running order.

What to look for in job ticket software

  • Automatic ticket creation from accepted quotes
  • Print-specific fields: stock, size, imposition, inks, finishing
  • File version control and proof approval linked to the ticket
  • A drag-and-drop production board with deadline alerts
  • Mobile access for the shop floor
  • Reporting on turnaround and on-time delivery
A good job ticket answers three questions instantly: what are we making, where is it now, and when is it due?

In Pulse Print, job tickets are created automatically from approved quotes and flow straight onto the live production board, so no job gets lost and every deadline is visible.
